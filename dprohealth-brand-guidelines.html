02 — Target Audience
Audience

Who We Serve

Men and women ages 30–55 who are successful in career or family life but feel like they're slowly losing control of how they look, feel, and perform.

💼
The Executive
High performer at work, but feeling the gap between career output and physical energy. Wants to lead from a position of strength — mentally and physically.
👨‍👩‍👧
The Busy Parent
Everyone else comes first. Sleep is compromised. The gym happens when it can. Wants to feel like themselves again and set the right example for their kids.
🎯
The Optimizer
Already doing the basics — sleeping, training, eating clean. But wants to go deeper. Peptides, hormones, longevity protocols. They'll pay for real expertise.
What They're Struggling With
Energy declining — the afternoon crash, waking unrefreshed, running on caffeine
Focus and mental edge slipping — brain fog, distractibility, can't think clearly
Hormones out of balance — doctor says "normal" but they feel far from it
Metabolism slowing — eating clean but body composition keeps shifting wrong
Confidence eroding — not recognizing themselves in the mirror or in the room
Recovery taking longer — injuries linger, soreness doesn't clear, body doesn't bounce back
Motivation flatlined — driven people who suddenly don't feel driven anymore
Information overload — drowning in conflicting advice, wants one trusted source
The Key Insight
This audience is successful, overwhelmed, and wants certainty. They will pay for clarity. Especially when you simplify things, educate well, sound confident, look the part, and have lived it. That's the exact profile of DPRO Health.

How We Sound

✦ Voice — We Do
Speak with authority — you've earned it through IFBB Pro status and lived experience
Simplify complex science without dumbing it down — explain the mechanism, then the practical application
Sound like insider information the audience wasn't supposed to know
Use specific data points, numbers, and mechanisms — they signal credibility
Address the person's frustration first, then give them the solution
Write conversationally — like a knowledgeable friend, not a textbook
Acknowledge what's hard before offering what works
✗ Voice — We Don't
Use cringe marketing language — "life-changing," "secret hack," "you won't believe"
Sound robotic or overly clinical — nobody wants to read a pharmacology lecture
Make medical claims or tell people what to do with their health — educate, don't prescribe
Use fear-based shame tactics around body image or health decline
Overuse jargon without explaining it — always follow a technical term with plain English
Promise overnight results — the audience is smart enough to know that's not real
Sound like every other supplement brand or fitness influencer

Logo Do's
Always maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "D" on all sides
Use only the approved color variations shown above
Use the icon mark for social avatars, favicons, and sub-40px applications
Always write "DPRO Health" — two words, DPRO in caps, Health title case
Logo Don'ts
Never distort, stretch, or rotate the wordmark
Never use unapproved colors or gradients on the logo
Never place the logo on a busy background without adequate contrast
Never separate "DPRO" from "Health" — they are one unit

09 — Platform Strategy
Distribution

Platform Strategy

Every platform has a specific role. Content is created with a primary platform in mind and distributed across others. Quality over quantity — every piece must meet the DPRO standard.

TikTok
Primary Discovery
Widest reach. 30–90 second science education. Hook-first, curiosity-driven. Highest volume platform.
Instagram
Authority & Trust
Reels + static posts. More polished. Audience skews slightly older. Strong for coaching conversions.
YouTube
Long-Term SEO
Shorts for discovery, long-form for depth. Evergreen content that compounds over time. Highest trust platform.
Facebook
35–55 Audience
Reels repurposed from TikTok. Strong reach with the exact target demographic. Underrated for this niche.
Email
Owned Audience
3,500+ subscribers. Weekly science-backed strategies. Highest conversion channel. Always build the list.
Content Format Strategy
All short-form content (30–90 seconds) follows the DPRO formula: Strong curiosity hook → mechanism or insight → practical application → implicit or explicit CTA. Content should sound like insider information — science-backed without being clinical, confident without being arrogant. Sound like Andrew Huberman's clarity meets the relatability of a coach who's lived it.
