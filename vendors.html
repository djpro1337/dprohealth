How I pick a vendor

I don't list every company that offers me a code. Here's what a vendor has to clear before it shows up on this page.

01
Third-party tested
I want to see a current COA from an independent lab — identity and purity, not just a label. If a vendor can't produce one on request, they don't make the list.
02
Consistent over time
One clean batch doesn't earn a spot. I look for product that performs the same way month after month — in my own use and in feedback from the community.
03
Handled properly
Peptides are fragile. Properly lyophilized vials, cold handling where it matters, fast shipping, and packaging that shows up intact every time.
